Performance

Wallet P&L moved to ClickHouse

15 min → <1 min99% of wallets

Heavy P&L reads caused a production outage. Rolling P&L on a 2B+ row Solana trades table stayed slow for wallets with millions of trades, even with indexes and per-token batching. I moved P&L analytics from PostgreSQL to ClickHouse.

Incident response

One wallet stalled Solana indexing

3B+ rowspipeline protected

Repeated P&L requests for one high-volume wallet overloaded the database and stopped indexing. I killed the running queries, disabled those reads, and limited P&L to wallets under a trade-count threshold using a precomputed trade-count table.
